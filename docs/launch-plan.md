# 30-day public-beta launch plan

All result fields begin as `Not yet measured`; update only from real links, interviews, and production aggregates.

| Window | Owner | Action | Campaign/link | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 0 | Product owner | Recruit 5–10 consenting friends/classmates; observe signup, onboarding, two stock views, and one advanced flow | Private links with `utm_campaign=private_alpha` | Not yet measured |
| Week 0 | Product owner | Capture five structured responses: expectation, confusion, trust, return reason, missing need | Feedback form/interview notes | Not yet measured |
| Week 1 | Product owner | Share testing request with permitted UTD engineering, finance, data-science, and entrepreneurship groups | Distinct campus UTM links | Not yet measured |
| Week 1 | Product owner | Publish concise LinkedIn build/architecture/methodology post with beta-testing request | `utm_source=linkedin&utm_campaign=campus_beta` | Not yet measured |
| Week 2 | Engineering | Publish point-in-time backtesting and anti-leakage write-up | `utm_campaign=anti_leakage_writeup` | Not yet measured |
| Week 2 | Product owner | Submit repository + live demo only where community self-promotion rules permit | Community-specific links | Not yet measured |
| Weeks 3–4 | Product + Engineering | Review counts for signup, verification, onboarding, activation, D7 return, views, backtests, shares, referrals, digest, feedback | Admin metrics/PostHog | Not yet measured |
| Weeks 3–4 | Product owner | Interview activated and non-activated users separately; fix the largest measured funnel drop | Changelog link | Not yet measured |

Never automate unsolicited outreach, scrape contacts, or turn research ranks into promotional return claims.
